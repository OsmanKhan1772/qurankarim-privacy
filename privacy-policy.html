Privacy Policy
Last Updated: May 28, 2026
Section 01

Introduction

Quran Kariim ("the App") is a free Islamic application developed by Ismaail Aweis Mohamed. We are committed to protecting the privacy of every user who uses this application.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your personal data. By using this app, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Section 02

Information We Collect

The Quran Kariim app collects minimal data necessary for the app to function properly.

  • Location data — Used only to calculate Prayer Times and Qiblah direction. This data is not stored on our servers.
  • Google account information — Only if you choose to sign in with Google (name, email). Used solely for account identification.
  • App preferences — Font size, dark mode, last read position, bookmarks. Stored locally on your device only.
  • Ad interaction data — Google AdMob may collect anonymous usage data for serving ads. See Google's privacy policy for details.

📌 We do NOT collect passwords, financial information, or any sensitive personal data.

Section 03

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to provide and improve the app's features:

  • To display accurate Prayer Times based on your location
  • To calculate the Qiblah direction toward Mecca
  • To save your reading progress and bookmarks locally
  • To display relevant advertisements through Google AdMob
  • To authenticate your account if you choose to sign in

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to any third parties.

Section 04

Third-Party Services

The app uses the following trusted third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — For displaying advertisements
  • Firebase / Google Sign-In — For optional user authentication
  • EveryAyah.com — For streaming Quran audio recitations
  • Islamic Network API — For Quran audio content
  • Al-Adhan API — For Prayer Times calculation
  • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim) — For location name lookup

Each of these services has its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review them.

Section 05

Data Storage & Security

All personal preferences (bookmarks, font size, theme, last read position) are stored locally on your device using SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly sign in with Google.

We take reasonable precautions to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Section 06

Children's Privacy

Quran Kariim is a family-friendly Islamic application suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us immediately and we will take steps to remove that information.

Section 07

App Permissions

The app requests the following device permissions:

  • INTERNET — To load Quran audio and prayer times
  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / COARSE_LOCATION — For prayer times and Qiblah only
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — For optional prayer time reminders
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM — For accurate prayer time notifications
  • VIBRATE — For notification alerts

You can revoke any of these permissions at any time from your device settings.

Section 08

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings
  • Revoke location permissions at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information below.

Section 09

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Continued use of the app after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
